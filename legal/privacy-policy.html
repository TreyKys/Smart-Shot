Privacy Policy for Sift

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Sift ("the App", "we", "us") is a screenshot organization app published by NeuroDevLabs. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it's processed, and your choices.

1. What Sift accesses on your device

With your permission, Sift reads the photos/screenshots on your device (Android's READ_MEDIA_IMAGES/READ_MEDIA_VIDEO permissions) so it can organize and tag them. Sift does not access any other files, contacts, location, or messages.

2. How your screenshots are processed

  • On-device text recognition: Sift uses Google's ML Kit to extract text from your screenshots directly on your device. This text never leaves your device during extraction.
  • AI tagging: To generate smart tags and categorize screenshots, Sift sends the screenshot image and/or extracted text to the Google Gemini API (Google's cloud AI service) for analysis. This is the only case where screenshot content leaves your device. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service.
  • Local storage: Screenshot metadata, tags, and app state are stored locally on your device in a local database. We do not operate a server that stores your screenshots or their contents.
  • Bring Your Own Key (BYOK): If you choose to supply your own Gemini API key in Settings, it is stored locally on your device only and is used to call Google's API directly; we never see it.

3. Advertising

Sift shows optional rewarded ads (via Google AdMob) that let you unlock additional AI processing. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, and may personalize ads based on your consent choices. If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you'll be shown a consent form before any personalized ads are requested, in line with Google's EU User Consent Policy. You can manage ad personalization in your device's ad settings at any time.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Sift Pro subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing and managed via RevenueCat, our subscription management provider. RevenueCat receives purchase/transaction data (not your screenshots) necessary to validate and manage your subscription. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

5. Notifications

Sift may show local notifications (e.g. daily digest, processing complete) generated entirely on your device. No notification content is sent to or from a server.

6. Data we do not collect

Sift does not require an account or login, does not collect your name or email address, and does not run any analytics or tracking SDK beyond what's described above.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your screenshots and tags remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App removes its local database. To request deletion of any data processed on Google's servers, see Google's own data controls.

8. Children's privacy

Sift is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at support@neurodevlabs.cloud.
